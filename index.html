Who we are

Small organisation, big European family

Papaya International is a non-governmental organisation from Bratislava, based in Piešťany. It was founded in 2026 by young people who experienced first-hand how a single Erasmus+ exchange can change the way you see the world – and yourself.

Today we send participants to projects all over Europe, host international groups in Slovakia and organise volunteering activities both in Slovakia and across Europe.

Mission & values

Mission

Give every young person – whatever their background or budget – the chance to learn, travel and make a difference.

Vision

A Europe where every young person feels confident, connected and able to shape their community.

Values
  • Inclusion – opportunities for everyone
  • Curiosity – new places, new views
  • Collaboration – stronger across borders
  • Impact – something lasting, every time
Why “Papaya”?

Bright on the outside, full of seeds on the inside – like the ideas we plant in every project.
