Who we are

Small organisation, big European family

Our mission

To give young people – regardless of background, budget or postcode – the chance to learn, travel, volunteer and make a real difference through international, non-formal education.

Our vision

A Europe where every young person feels confident, connected and able to shape the community they live in.

What we stand for

  • Inclusion

    Opportunities for everyone, especially young people with fewer opportunities.

  • Curiosity

    We learn by exploring new places, cultures and points of view.

  • Collaboration

    Strong partnerships across borders make projects better.

  • Impact

    Every project should leave something lasting behind – skills, friendships, change.

Our story

Papaya International was founded in Piešťany by a group of young people who experienced first-hand how a single Erasmus+ exchange can change the way you see the world – and yourself. We wanted more young Slovaks to have that chance.

Today we prepare and send participants to projects all over Europe, host international groups in Slovakia and run our own educational programmes in schools. [ADD: founding year and a milestone or two.]

Why “Papaya”? Bright on the outside, full of seeds on the inside – just like the ideas we plant in every project.

What we do

Three pillars, one goal

Everything we do helps young people grow into active, open-minded Europeans.

01

Erasmus+

We organise and co-organise youth exchanges (KA152) and mobility of youth workers (KA153), and send Slovak participants to projects run by our partners across Europe.

02

Youth participation

We help young people find their voice – through volunteering, local initiatives and dialogue with decision-makers – so they can actively shape their communities.

03

Non-formal education

Workshops, simulations and trainings where learning happens by doing – in schools, youth centres and international groups – on topics like media literacy, economics and sustainability.
